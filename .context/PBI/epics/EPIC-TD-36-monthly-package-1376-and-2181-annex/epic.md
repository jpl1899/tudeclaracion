## Description

## Epic goal

For a company and a period, generate and view the 2181 and the 1376 together, because the 2181 is the mandatory annex of the 1376 in the monthly DGI filing.

## Notes

Reuses the declaracion period entity (ADR-0003) as the aggregation root: a period's declaracion can carry the 2181, the 1376, or both. The data-model detail of how the pair links is /sprint-development work.
